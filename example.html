What's At Stake & What's Next

Egypt: Win = group winners + first-ever knockout berth. Draw = guaranteed top-2 (advances), full stop — the only 'unless Belgium win by 3+' caveat decides 1st vs 2nd place/seeding, NOT survival. Lose = need to scoreboard-watch Vancouver.

Iran: Win = essentially through (~5 pts), tops group if Belgium don't win. Draw = 3 pts but likely not enough; relies on best-third math (8 of 12 thirds advance) + the NZ-Belgium result. Lose = out.

Played in parallel: New Zealand vs Belgium (Vancouver) kicks off simultaneously.

Fan shortcut: Egypt fans only need to glance at Vancouver if Iran are winning in Seattle. Iran fans: win and you're in.

Salah vs his own manager's record: Coach Hossam Hassan IS the 69-goal record-holder Salah (68) is chasing — on Hassan's own bench. A goal tonight equals it.

Egypt's first knockout berth in 92 years: First Arab/African nation at a World Cup (1934); never advanced. A draw makes history.

What To Watch For

Salah vs Iran's left sideCutting in off the right, Salah meets a double-team in the low block. Watch if he drifts central to combine with Marmoush rather than forcing the 1v1 — and if RB Hany gets overlap space when Iran narrow.
Taremi vs Egypt's centre-backsThe marquee duel. Iran's everything — focal point in open play AND on set pieces, but largely isolated. Watch him drop to drag a CB out; that gap is Iran's best route in.
Egypt's double pivot vs Iran's counter-runnersThe control battle. Egypt dominate the ball; the instant they lose it, Iran spring Ghoddos & Jahanbakhsh into the space the full-backs vacate. Snuff the first counter pass and Iran starve.
Marmoush's pace vs a 30-something back lineCan veteran defenders handle runners in behind for 90 minutes? Late game is where this opens up.
Egypt dead-ball: Salah is the dead-ball weapon (direct FKs, corners, penalties); Marmoush/Zizo on delivery. Danger is delivery quality + Salah's boot more than a towering target.
Iran dead-ball: A clear scoring route. Aerial targets Kanaani & Khalilzadeh plus Taremi attack corners/wide FKs (taken by Ghoddos, Taremi, Hosseinzadeh).
Set-piece tip: If it's 0-0 near the hour, treat every Iranian corner or wide free kick as a genuine chance — their most realistic path to a goal.
