Champions · How The Final Was Won

Spain, world champions for the second time. Sixteen years after Iniesta's goal in 2010, La Roja are back on top — the crowning act of a golden generation that already had Euro 2024 and the 2023 Nations League. Goalless through 90 and deep into extra time, the final turned on Ferran Torres' 106th-minute winner off the bench, and Spain didn't blink.

Argentina, runners-up. The dream of back-to-back titles — unmatched since Brazil in 1962 — ended one goal short in extra time. Battle-hardened by a knockout run of narrow escapes, they had nothing left to answer Spain's decisive strike.

Messi's last dance. At 39, in his sixth and final World Cup, the all-time record-holder for appearances (30) and goals (20) walked off with a runners-up medal. The greatest career, one last final — this time it wasn't to be.

The meanest defence in the tournament. Spain conceded just once in the whole knockout phase (to Belgium) and kept another clean sheet in the final. Unai Simón's shutout run was the spine of the title.

A generational handover. Lamine Yamal (19) ends the summer a world champion; Messi (39) bows out. The outgoing GOAT and the incoming face of the sport, in the same showpiece.

What Decided It

Yamal vs Argentina's left sideThe tournament's brightest teenager against Tagliafico and a doubled-up Argentine left. Denied the clean 1v1s he'd feasted on all summer, whether Yamal could conjure a single moment was the final's headline sub-plot.
Messi vs Rodri & Spain's screenMessi roaming for pockets against a Spain side built to control the middle. Rodri — fit again, a Ballon d'Or anchor — shielding the back four and cutting the supply. Spain's answer: keep the ball, starve the No. 10.
The midfield chess matchPedri, Rodri & Olmo vs Enzo, Mac Allister & De Paul — two world-class engine rooms. Whoever owned the centre owned the final; Spain's control eventually told, and the one goal fell their way.
The goalkeepersUnai Simón vs Dibu Martínez — a final of few clear chances turned on the two keepers, both imperious. Dibu, Argentina's 2022 folk hero; Simón, owner of the tournament's stingiest defence — and the one who kept his clean sheet to the end while Spain found the winner.
How the margin fell Spain's way: in extra time, with legs gone and Argentina's knockout mileage (three ties to extra time or the wire) telling, Spain's fresher route showed — de la Fuente went to his bench and Ferran Torres delivered the 106th-minute winner to crown them champions.
