## What To Watch For
- Al-Tamari vs Argentina's rotated flank: Jordan's one European-level player (Rennes, Ligue 1) is quick and direct. With Argentina shuffling the back line, the full-back in front of him won't be first-choice — Jordan's single most likely route to a moment. Watch if he's let run at an unfamiliar defender.
- Olwan vs Argentina's reserve centre-backs: Scorer of Jordan's first-ever WC goal, against a patched-together back line with little rhythm together. His movement and poacher's timing are Jordan's best hope of a famous second goal.
- Argentina's reserve creators vs Jordan's low block: Can a second-string Argentina break down the disciplined, compact defence that nearly shocked Asia in 2024? Jordan will sit deep — patience and quality vs organisation and bodies.
- The Messi cameo vs a tiring Jordan: The night's emotional pivot. A second-half Messi introduction against a debutant nation, chasing more WC history, is the most likely "moment everyone remembers." Watch the bench.
- Argentina dead-ball: With Messi benched, on-pitch penalty taker likely Lautaro Martínez or Julián Álvarez; direct free-kicks revert to Messi the instant he's on. Not primarily a set-piece side — threat is open-play combination.
- Jordan dead-ball: A genuine weapon and central to the Asian Cup run. Al-Tamari is the chief delivery man; captain Ehsan Haddad is the aerial target in the box. (Penalty taker unconfirmed with Al-Naimat injured out.)
- Set-piece tip: If Jordan win a wide free kick or corner in Argentina's half, watch Al-Tamari's delivery onto Haddad — the one phase where the quality gulf narrows against a rotated, less-drilled defensive unit.
