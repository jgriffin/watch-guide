What's At Stake & What's Next

USA: Win = first-ever World Cup win over Belgium since 1930, and a quarter-final on home soil. A raucous Seattle crowd, a young Pochettino side, and a shot at the last eight.

Belgium: Win = the golden generation rolls on — De Bruyne, Lukaku, Tielemans and co. into a quarter-final, quite possibly their last major-tournament run together.

Winner's reward: a Quarter-final on Fri Jul 10 vs the winner of Portugal vs Spain — an Iberian heavyweight awaits.

The 2014 ghost: the last USA–Belgium meeting was the 2-1 AET heartbreak in Salvador. Every USMNT fan in the building will remember Tim Howard's 15 saves.

1930 bookend: the only time the USA has ever beaten Belgium was a 3-0 win in 1930 — the USMNT's first-ever World Cup match. This is a chance to end a ~96-year wait.

What To Watch For

Pulisic vs Belgium's right sideThe headline duel. The USMNT's talisman and top scorer cuts in from the left; Castagne (and whoever partners him) must live with him for 90 minutes. Watch whether Pulisic isolates 1v1 or drifts central to combine.
Doku vs Antonee RobinsonArguably the best footrace on the pitch — Doku's dribbling and burst against one of the fastest full-backs in world football. Whoever wins this flank tilts the game.
De Bruyne vs Adams & the USA midfieldThe control battle. Tyler Adams is the USA's screen and ball-winner; his job is to deny De Bruyne the half-second he needs. Every KDB touch between the lines is danger.
USA's press vs Belgium's veteran build-upPochettino's side want to hunt the ball high; Belgium, with Courtois behind and De Bruyne dropping in, want to play through it. If the press breaks, Doku and Trossard run at a stretched back line.
USA dead-ball: Pulisic is the delivery + direct free-kick threat; the danger is speed of transition (Weah, Pepi runs) more than aerial height.
Belgium dead-ball: a clear edge — Courtois (6'6") commands his box, De Bruyne's delivery is elite, and Lukaku off the bench is a genuine late target man.
Set-piece tip: if it's tight past the hour, watch for Garcia to send on Lukaku and turn crosses and set-pieces into Belgium's cleanest route to a goal.
