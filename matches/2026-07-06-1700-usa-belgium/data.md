## What To Watch For
- **Christian Pulisic (USA, 10) vs Belgium's right side (Castagne / De Winter)** — the match's headline duel. The USMNT captain-in-spirit and top scorer cuts in from the left; Belgium's right-back must live with him for 90 minutes. Watch whether Pulisic isolates 1v1 or drifts central to combine.
- **Jérémy Doku (BEL, 11) vs Antonee Robinson (USA, 5)** — arguably the best individual footrace on the pitch: Doku's dribbling and burst against one of the fastest full-backs in world football. Whoever wins this flank tilts the game.
- **Kevin De Bruyne (BEL, 7) vs Tyler Adams (USA, 4) & the USA midfield** — the control battle. Adams is USA's screen and ball-winner; his job is to deny De Bruyne the half-second he needs to unlock a defense. Every KDB touch between the lines is danger.
- **USA's press vs Belgium's veteran build-up (Courtois distribution)** — Pochettino's side want to hunt the ball high; Belgium, with Courtois, Witsel-era calm and De Bruyne dropping, want to play through it. If USA's press breaks, Doku and Trossard run at a stretched back line.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball:** Belgium hold the aerial edge — Courtois (6'6") commands his box, and **Lukaku off the bench is a genuine target-man weapon late**; De Bruyne's delivery is elite. USA lean on Pulisic's dead-ball delivery and quick counters (Weah, Pepi runs). **Tip:** if it's tight past the hour, watch for Garcia to send on Lukaku and turn crosses/set-pieces into Belgium's cleanest route to a goal.

## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **A 2014 rematch, 12 years on.** These teams last met in the 2014 World Cup R16, where Belgium won 2-1 AET behind **Tim Howard's record 15 saves** — still the most in a World Cup match on record. De Bruyne and Lukaku scored that day and are both here again.
- **The co-hosts at home.** USA are 2026 co-hosts, riding a Seattle home crowd into a knockout tie at Lumen Field — a rare home-soil World Cup R16 for the USMNT.
- **The only time USA has beaten Belgium was 1930** — a 3-0 win in the USA's first-ever World Cup match. A win here would be their first over Belgium in ~96 years.
- **Belgium's golden generation, last dance.** De Bruyne (34), Lukaku (33), Witsel (37) and co. are almost certainly on their final major-tournament run — and still delivering (Tielemans' 120th-minute R32 winner, Lukaku's late goals).
- **Tielemans the R32 folk hero:** forced extra time vs Senegal at 89', then won it from the penalty spot deep in extra time (120+5').
- **Balogun's breakout:** the Monaco striker has 3 goals this World Cup (a brace vs Paraguay, the opener vs Bosnia) after choosing the USA over England and France.
- **A true coin-flip:** books price it a straight pick 'em — one of the most evenly matched ties of the Round of 16.
- **Pochettino vs Garcia:** two well-traveled European club managers on the touchlines — Pochettino the first South American-born USMNT boss, Garcia steering Belgium's transition.
- **Belgium's keeper wall:** Courtois (6'6") is one of the tournament's standout goalkeepers; USA counter with Matt Freese, the shootout-tested NYCFC keeper who has leapfrogged Matt Turner as the No.1 in-tournament.
