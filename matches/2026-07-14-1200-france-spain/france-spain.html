Match101 · Semi-final
Spain reached their first World Cup final since they won it in 2010. France, unbeaten and scoring in every game, were held scoreless for the first time all tournament — Deschamps' farewell run ends in the semi.

What It Meant & What's Next

Spain: back in a World Cup final for the first time since 2010 — the year of their only title. They arrived unbeaten, had conceded just once (to Belgium), and answered with a clean sheet against the tournament's most dangerous attack. Next: the FINAL vs Argentina.

France: the end of Deschamps' farewell run and any hope of back-to-back finals. Les Bleus had won every match and scored in every match until this one; the shutout ended both streaks. Next: the third-place playoff vs England.

Two Ballon d'Or winners couldn't tip it: Dembélé (2025) started for France, Rodri (2024) anchored Spain — the possession side got the reward.

Yamal, 19, capped the run that lit up the tournament with the goal that sent Spain through.

What Decided It

Rodri & Pedri vs Tchouaméni & RabiotThe real battleground. Spain's positional game wanted the ball and the middle — and got both, dictating tempo and squeezing France's supply lines. Own the centre, own the game; Spain did.
Mbappé vs Cubarsí & LaporteFrance's talisman against a composed young back line. Spain's plan was to starve him of clean service and dare France to break them down. It held: France 0.
Lamine Yamal vs France's leftSpain's teenage talisman isolated France's full-back in 1v1s; the space in behind was where the second goal grew.
Dembélé & Olise vs Spain's pressThe shutout-breakers France needed. But Spain's ball-dominance kept Les Bleus chasing, and the platform to turn Spain's high line never came.
The set-piece that never came: Spain barely conceded from open play all tournament, so France's cheapest route was a dead ball — Mbappé's boot, an aerial box. Spain's own control meant they rarely had to defend one.
