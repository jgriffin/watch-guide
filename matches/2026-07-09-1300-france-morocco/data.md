- **Mbappé vs Morocco's back line (Diop/Riad, Aguerd pushing to start).** France's talisman on 7 goals against one of the tournament's best-drilled defenses. Morocco will sit compact and dare France to break them down — exactly the puzzle Paraguay set for 70 minutes in the R16.

- **Depth as a weapon.** France's edge in the R16 was the bench — Doué came on and manufactured the winner. Ouahbi (2025 U-20 World Cup winner) has trusted a young core; late in a tight knockout, France's substitutes are elite.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball:** **Morocco carry a clear dead-ball edge** — **Hakimi on penalties** (panenka to eliminate Spain in 2022; scored the decisive kick in the 2026 R32 shootout vs the Netherlands), plus aerial presence in **Aguerd/Diop** off corners. **France:** Mbappé (penalties), set-piece delivery from the pivot; a physical, athletic box. If it's level late, a spot-kick shootout would favor Morocco's nerve and Hakimi's record.
