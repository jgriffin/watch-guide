What's At Stake & What's Next

France: A win puts them in a semi-final and one step from back-to-back finals — all in Deschamps' farewell tournament (he steps down after 2026). They've won all five so far, a France record for a single World Cup.

Morocco: The 2022 semifinalists want to go one better — and settle a score. Beat France and it's a second straight run to the last four, this time as the story of a new, young regime under Ouahbi.

Next: the winner meets the winner of Spain vs Belgium in the Semi-final, Tue Jul 14 at AT&T Stadium, Arlington.

Revenge angle: Morocco haven't forgotten Qatar. Théo Hernández (still in this France squad) scored inside 5 minutes in that 2022 semi.

Mbappé's milestone: on 7 goals, co-leading the Golden Boot race, and already France's all-time top scorer.

What To Watch For

Hakimi vs France's left sideThe tie's headline duel. Morocco's captain attacks like a winger and is their biggest outlet; France's left-back (Digne, with Theo an option) must defend him and support the attack. Extra spice: Hakimi's day job is at PSG — in France.
Mbappé vs Morocco's back lineFrance's talisman against one of the tournament's best-drilled defenses. Morocco will sit compact and dare France to break them down — the exact puzzle Paraguay set for 70 minutes in the R16.
France's control vs Morocco's press & countersMorocco's young, high-energy midfield swarmed Canada's first pass. If they unsettle France's build-up and spring Brahim Díaz on the break, this becomes a real contest rather than a siege.
The benchesFrance's edge in the R16 was depth — Doué came on and manufactured the winner. Late in a tight knockout, France's substitutes are elite; Ouahbi's answer is a fearless young core.
Morocco dead-ball: A clear edge — Hakimi on penalties (2022 panenka vs Spain; the winning kick in the 2026 R32 shootout vs the Netherlands), plus Aguerd/Diop aerial threat off corners.
France: Mbappé on penalties; delivery from the pivot into a big, athletic box.
Set-piece tip: If it's level late, don't fear extra time on Morocco's behalf — they beat the Netherlands on penalties this tournament and Spain on penalties in 2022. In a shootout, Hakimi's nerve is a genuine advantage.
