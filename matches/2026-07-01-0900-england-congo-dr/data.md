## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **First World Cup in 52 years.** DR Congo's only previous appearance was **1974 in West Germany, as Zaire** — this is a once-in-a-generation return, and their **first-ever World Cup knockout appearance** (Zaire lost all three in 1974).
- **First-ever WC goal — 52 years late.** Wissa's strike vs Portugal was the **first goal DR Congo/Zaire has ever scored at a World Cup** (Zaire scored zero in 1974, conceding 14 including a famous **0–9 loss to Yugoslavia**).
- **The Mwepu Ilunga moment.** Zaire 1974 produced one of football's most famous oddities: defender **Mwepu Ilunga** breaking from the wall to boot a Brazil free-kick away *before* the whistle — bewildering commentators for decades.
- **A diaspora squad.** Almost the entire 26 plays in Europe and many were born there — **Wan-Bissaka switched from England to DR Congo** (FIFA approved Aug 2025), and Premier League names (Wissa, Tuanzebe, Sadiki, E. Kayembe) dot the roster.
- **Snuck through, now dangerous.** DR Congo advanced from a brutal group (Colombia + Portugal) on just 4 points as a best-third — the classic knockout underdog.
- **Atlanta connection.** Atlanta's large Congolese community has rallied around the Leopards; a local megachurch publicly honoured the squad — a home-from-home for a game played in Georgia.
- **Kane on the climb.** Harry Kane just passed Gary Lineker (10) to become **England's all-time World Cup top scorer with 11** — and England are at their **8th consecutive World Cup**.
- **Mismatch on paper.** England (FIFA #4, −370) vs a side that the bookmakers price at +1200 — but knockout football, a five-man Congolese block and Wissa's pace make it a live banana-skin.
