## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **First-ever France–Sweden World Cup meeting** — 96 years after the inaugural 1930 tournament. (All-time H2H ~24 games, France lead ~12-6.)
- **~£190m strike pair vs the world finalists:** Isak (£125m British record) + Gyökeres (~£64m) are the most expensive forward line at the tournament — facing the 2018 winners / 2022 runners-up.
- **Both strikers flopped early at their new clubs** — Isak and Gyökeres had a thin Premier League start; the World Cup has been their reset. They'd barely played together for Sweden before this tournament.
- **Dembélé's first-half hat-trick vs Norway** — first at a World Cup since Oleg Salenko (1994); only the 3rd by a Frenchman ever (after Fontaine and Mbappé).
- **Deschamps' last dance** — his final tournament; chasing a 3rd World Cup (1998 as captain, 2018 as coach). Zidane is the widely-tipped successor (*reported, not official — flag*).
- **An Englishman managing Sweden** — Graham Potter was hired Oct 2025 only weeks after Tomasson was sacked mid-qualifying; Sweden squeaked in via the March 2026 playoffs (beat Ukraine).
- **Defensive gulf:** France conceded *one* goal in the group; Sweden shipped *five* to the Netherlands in a single game.
- **Mbappé the penalty king** — tied Messi for the most penalty goals in World Cup qualifying.
- **Past flashpoints:** Zlatan Ibrahimović's stunning volley in Sweden's 2-0 Euro 2012 win; a home-and-home 2-1 split in 2018 WC qualifying; France won the last meeting 4-2 (Nations League, Nov 2020).
- **Paraguay's giant-killing** (Germany out on pens) means the winner here dodges every superpower until a likely quarter-final.
