- **Salah vs Australia's right side (Geria / Souttar)** — IF FIT (see fitness flag). Egypt's whole attack runs through Salah drifting in off the right; Australia's low block funnels him into Souttar and the RB. The game's defining duel — and its biggest unknown.
- **Australia's aerial set-pieces vs Egypt's box** — Australia are thin in open play (2 goals in 3 games) but carry a tall, dangerous set-piece presence: Souttar (6'6"), Burgess, Degenek. In a tight game, dead balls are their cheapest route to a goal.
- **Irvine & Metcalfe vs Egypt's Ashour/Fathy pivot** — the control battle. Australia's St. Pauli midfield engine runs the press and second balls; Egypt want to edge it centrally and feed Salah. Whoever owns the middle owns the tempo.
- **Irankunda's pace on the counter vs Egypt's pushed-up full-backs** — Egypt commit Fatouh/Hany forward; the 20-year-old Irankunda (plus Leckie wide) is Australia's transition threat into the space they vacate.
- **Set-piece tip:** Two cagey, defense-first sides + an Under-2.5 lean → a dead ball may well decide it. Watch Souttar on every Australian corner, and Salah's right boot on any Egyptian free kick.
- **Dead-ball takers:** Egypt — Salah (direct FKs, corners, penalties). Australia — usual taker Goodwin is OUT of the squad; likeliest now Mabil or Irvine (unconfirmed — flag).
