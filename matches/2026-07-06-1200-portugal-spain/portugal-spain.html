What's At Stake & What's Next

Portugal: Win = the quarter-finals — and a first-ever World Cup victory over Spain, at the perfect moment. It's also Roberto Martínez's farewell run: he leaves after this tournament regardless of the result.

Spain: Arrived unbeaten and yet to concede — 8 goals scored, 0 against. The reigning European champions are chasing the one trophy still missing since Euro 2024.

Next: the winner goes to the Quarter-final on Fri Jul 10, vs the winner of USA vs Belgium.

Ronaldo's milestone: at 41, this is his sixth World Cup; he's 2 goals from passing Eusébio's Portugal World Cup record (9).

The subplot in the home dugout: Portugal are coached by a Spaniard — Roberto Martínez, born in Catalonia — trying to knock out his own homeland.

What To Watch For

Nuno Mendes vs Lamine YamalThe marquee duel. Mendes shut Yamal down in the 2025 Nations League final — the rematch may decide the tie. Mendes is an attacking full-back who pushes high; the space behind him is exactly where Spain will try to spring Yamal in transition.
The midfield: Rodri & Pedri vs Vitinha & João NevesSpain's positional game wants to overload and play through the middle; previews flag Portugal's pivot as vulnerable to central penetration. Whoever owns the centre likely owns the game.
Bruno Fernandes vs Spain's pressPortugal's chief creator against a ball-dominant, high-pressing side. If Bruno can turn and release Leão or Ronaldo, that's Portugal's clearest route in.
Ronaldo vs Cubarsí & LaporteWith Spain likely to hold the ball, Portugal's threat is direct and transitional — Ronaldo living on the shoulder of Spain's young back line, waiting for one gap.
Portugal dead-ball: With Spain barely conceding in open play, a set piece may be Portugal's cheapest goal — Bruno Fernandes on delivery, Ronaldo's boot the direct threat.
Spain: Patient possession and overloads rather than dead balls — they'll try to walk it in, not launch it.
Set-piece tip: Two of the last three Portugal–Spain knockouts went to penalties (Euro 2012 to Spain, 2025 Nations League final to Portugal). If it's level late, expect this one to go the distance too.
