## What To Watch For
- **Nuno Mendes (POR, 25) vs Lamine Yamal (ESP, 19)** — the marquee duel. Mendes **shut Yamal down in the 2025 Nations League final**; the rematch may decide the tie. Mendes is an attacking full-back who pushes high — the space behind him is exactly where Spain will try to spring Yamal in transition.
- **Midfield control: Rodri/Pedri (ESP) vs Vitinha/João Neves (POR)** — Spain's positional game wants to overload and play through the middle; previews flag Vitinha's side as vulnerable to central penetration. Whoever owns the centre likely owns the game.
- **Bruno Fernandes (POR, 8) vs Spain's press** — Portugal's chief creator against a ball-dominant, high-pressing side. If Bruno can turn and release Leão or Ronaldo, that's Portugal's route in.
- **Ronaldo (POR, 7) vs Cubarsí/Laporte (ESP)** — with Spain likely to hold the ball, Portugal's threat is more direct and transitional; Ronaldo living on the shoulder of Spain's young back line.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball tip:** Spain barely concede from open play, so Portugal's cheapest goal may be a **dead ball** (Bruno's delivery, Ronaldo's boot) or a **Leão break** in transition. Two of the last three Portugal-Spain knockouts went to **penalties** — if it's level late, expect it to go the distance.
