What's At Stake & What's Next

Winner takes all: straight to the Round of 16 — vs the winner of Netherlands vs Morocco, in Houston (NRG Stadium) on July 4. Loser is eliminated.

History for one of them: neither Canada nor South Africa has ever reached a World Cup knockout round before tonight — whoever wins makes their nation's deepest-ever run.

Co-hosts on the road: Canada are tournament co-hosts but finished 2nd in Group B, which sent them to LA rather than a home venue — a "home World Cup, away knockout."

Broos' last dance: South Africa's 74-year-old Belgian coach retires after this tournament and is already the oldest manager ever to reach a World Cup knockout stage.

The penalty subplot: with a ~24% draw probability, keeper Ronwen Williams' 4-save AFCON shootout heroics loom over any tie that goes the distance.

Davies watch: Canada's captain has played zero minutes all tournament — if he gets off the bench, it's the emotional beat of the night.

What To Watch For

Jonathan David vs SA's CBs + WilliamsCanada's talisman (3 goals already this World Cup) against a young, organized Bafana back line — Okon & Mbokazi — that conceded little but created almost nothing. One clean sight of goal likely wins it; if Williams stands tall, this drifts toward penalties.
SA's counter vs Canada's full-backsBroos' whole plan is low-block-then-spring. Maseko, Mofokeng & Appollis are quick in transition and will target the space Laryea & Johnston vacate. Snuff the first break and SA starve.
Midfield: Mokoena & Mbatha vs Saliba & ChoinièreWhoever owns the middle third decides the night — a Canada siege, or an end-to-end counter-fest. Young Nathan Saliba is Canada's metronome; Mokoena is SA's set-piece and territory man.
If he appears: Davies vs MudauThe matchup everyone wants — Canada's captain's pace down the left against SA right-back Khuliso Mudau. Only if Davies' fitness allows a cameo.
Set-piece tip: South Africa are the bigger dead-ball threat. Teboho Mokoena takes the free kicks AND penalties (he scored the WC penalty vs Czechia and a famous AFCON free kick), with Makgopa as the aerial target. For a side that creates so little in open play, treat every SA set piece as a genuine chance. Canada's aerial target is Larin; David is the likely penalty taker (inferred).
