## What To Watch For
- **Jonathan David vs South Africa's centre-backs (Okon & Mbokazi) + Williams.** David's movement/finishing against a young, organized back line that conceded little but created almost nothing. One clean sight and Canada likely win; if Williams stands tall, this drags toward penalties.
- **South Africa's transition speed (Maseko / Mofokeng / Appollis) vs Canada's full-backs (Laryea & Johnston).** Broos' plan is low-block-then-counter; the break is SA's only real route to goal, and it targets the space Canada's full-backs vacate.
- **Midfield control: Mokoena & Mbatha vs Saliba & Choinière.** Whoever owns this third decides whether it's a Canada siege or an end-to-end counter-fest.
- **(If he appears) Alphonso Davies vs Khuliso Mudau (SA RB)** — a pure pace duel down Canada's left, the matchup everyone wants if Davies gets off the bench.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball tip:** South Africa are the bigger dead-ball threat. **Teboho Mokoena is the designated free-kick AND penalty taker** (WC penalty vs Czechia; AFCON free-kick vs Morocco) with **Makgopa** the aerial target — for a side that creates so little in open play, set pieces are a primary scoring avenue. ## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **First-ever competitive meeting** between the nations. Only prior meeting: a 2007 friendly, **South Africa won 2-0** — Canada have never beaten South Africa.
- **Neither nation has ever played a World Cup knockout match** — first time for both; one makes history tonight.
- **Hugo Broos (74)** becomes the **oldest manager ever to lead a side in a World Cup knockout stage** — and **retires after this tournament** (won AFCON 2017 with Cameroon).
- South Africa's **last 11 World Cup goals were scored by 11 different players** — no repeat scorer.
- **Canada advanced with just 4 points**, and **6 of their group goals came in one game** — the 6-0 rout of Qatar that was their **first-ever World Cup win**.
- South Africa reached the last 32 with the **fewest opposition-box touches and big chances created** of any qualifier — efficiency and defense over creation.
- **Canada have lost both previous competitive matches against African nations** (Cameroon 2001, Morocco 2022) — can they finally beat one?
- **Jonathan David** is Canada's all-time men's top scorer (39) with 3 at this World Cup; **Maseko's 63' winner vs South Korea** is the goal that put Bafana here.
- Williams' **4-save shootout** at AFCON 2023 looms over any tie that reaches penalties (~24% draw probability).
- SoFi Stadium has a **fixed roof** — weather is a non-factor (mild ~72°F outside).
