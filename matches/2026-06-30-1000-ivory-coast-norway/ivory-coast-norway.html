What's At Stake & What's Next

Ivory Coast: Win = a first-ever World Cup knockout-round victory and the Round of 16 — uncharted territory for the Elephants. Reaching R32 is already their best-ever WC run; this is pure house money.

Norway: Win = the Round of 16, matching the spirit of their only previous knockout appearance (France '98). Lose = a long-awaited golden generation's World Cup ends at the first knockout hurdle.

The reward: the winner runs straight into Brazil in the Round of 16 (Sun Jul 5) — Brazil edged Japan 2-1 on a Gabriel Martinelli stoppage-time winner.

Haaland watch: 4 goals in 2 games at his first-ever World Cup; already the first Norwegian to score in consecutive WC matches. This is his first WC do-or-die.

Kessié's encore: Ivory Coast's captain scored the equalizer in the 2023 AFCON final and again vs Germany here — and he's on penalties if it goes the distance.

What To Watch For

Haaland vs Ivory Coast's blockThe match in one duel. Haaland thrives on minimal service (4 goals in 2); Ivory Coast's whole identity is being hard to break down. Watch their centre-backs (Ousmane Diomande & Agbadou) and GK Fofana track his movement on the shoulder — one lapse is all he needs.
Ødegaard vs IC's midfield screenNorway's creativity runs almost entirely through their captain. If Kessié, Sangaré & Oulaï smother Ødegaard, the supply to Haaland dries up and Norway become one-dimensional — set pieces and long balls only.
Width & the counterAntonio Nusa and Norway's pace test IC's full-backs; the other way, Ivory Coast are dangerous on the break through Amad Diallo and Yan Diomande. Sub-plot: Yan Diomande vs Nusa are RB Leipzig teammates facing off. IC's Singo is doubtful (hamstring), thinning their athletic full-back cover.
Whose flaw breaks firstNorway leaked 7 goals in the group (France put four past them); Ivory Coast's finishing has been wasteful all tournament despite scoring first in every game. The side that fixes its weakness likely advances.
Norway dead-ball: built to punish them — Ødegaard delivery to Haaland is a primary route; Haaland takes pens, Ødegaard & Ryerson on free kicks/corners (Ryerson's thigh is a watch).
Ivory Coast dead-ball: Kessié penalties (Sangaré backup), Amad Diallo & Kessié free kicks, Pépé / Yan Diomande / Adingra corners.
Set-piece tip: Norway are the more dangerous dead-ball side — in a tight game, treat every Norwegian corner or wide free kick as a genuine chance, with Haaland the target. It may be how they unlock a packed Ivorian box.
