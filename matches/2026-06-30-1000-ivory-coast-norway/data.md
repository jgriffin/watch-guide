## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **Haaland is the first Norwegian to score in consecutive World Cup matches** — and only the 2nd player in 50 years (after Harry Kane '18) with multiple goals in each of his first two WC games.
- This is **Haaland's first World Cup, full stop** — generational striker, never played one until age 25.
- **Ødegaard has assisted Haaland 9 times** for Norway — more than any other teammate in Haaland's international career.
- **Norway's first World Cup since France 1998** — a 28-year absence; their only previous knockout appearance was the 1998 Round of 16.
- Norway **qualified with a perfect 8-from-8** record (37 scored, 5 conceded); led at half-time in both group wins.
- **Ivory Coast reached the Round of 32 for the first time in their history** — and **scored first in all three group games** (rare).
- **Ivory Coast are the reigning African champions** (AFCON 2023, won as hosts; Kessié scored in the final vs Nigeria) — under Emerse Faé, who took over mid-tournament as a caretaker and won it.
- **First-ever meeting between the nations** — and a do-or-die knockout for both.
- **AT&T Stadium hosts more 2026 matches than any other venue (9)** and is expected to draw the tournament's biggest crowds (~94,000); its retractable roof + A/C neutralize a 96°F Texas afternoon.
- Whoever wins runs straight into **Brazil** in the Round of 16.
