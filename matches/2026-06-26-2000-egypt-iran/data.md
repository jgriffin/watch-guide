## What To Watch For
- Salah (central No.10) vs Ezatolahi: Hossam Hassan has moved Salah off the wing into the centre — 1 goal + 2 assists already in the role. Iran's holding midfielder Saeid Ezatolahi screens exactly the pockets Salah now haunts. The game's most dangerous player vs the man tasked with erasing him.
- Taremi vs Egypt's reshuffled CBs: With Hamdy Fathy injured, Egypt patch their back line (Yasser Ibrahim / Ramy Rabia). Taremi lives on runs in behind and 1v1 finishing — a noted penalty-winner. Egypt must stay disciplined; one lapse is Iran's whole game plan.
- Marmoush's pace vs Iran's veteran back line: Iran started the oldest XI in World Cup history last time out. Marmoush running in transition at a back three RotoWire flatly calls slow is Egypt's clearest route to a goal — especially late.
- Egypt's pivot vs Iran's counters: Attia & Lasheen must win second balls and snuff the first counter-pass. Lose the ball cheaply and Iran spring Jahanbakhsh & Mohebi into the space the full-backs vacate — Iran's only real way through.
- Egypt dead-ball: Salah is the hub — direct free kicks, corners, penalties; Marmoush and Zizo also deliver. Threat is delivery quality and Salah's right boot more than aerial size.
- Iran dead-ball: their most realistic goal source. Corners from Ghoddos/Hosseinzadeh, free kicks from Taremi & Ghoddos, penalties Taremi. A tall, experienced squad that backs itself in the box — Beiranvand's long throw is a bonus attacking outlet.
- Set-piece tip: if it's level near the hour, treat every Iranian corner or wide free kick as a genuine chance — the likeliest way this game's deadlock breaks.
