What's At Stake & What's Next

England: Already into the Round of 32. A win = guaranteed top of Group L and a friendlier knockout route. This game is about seeding, not survival — even a slip keeps them through.

Panama: Mathematically out. The prize now is a first-ever World Cup point and only their second-ever World Cup goal — and a last dance for a squad built on the 2018 pioneers.

Played in parallel: Croatia vs Ghana (Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia) decides the rest of the group — Ghana advance with a draw; Croatia likely need the win.

Kane on the line: Harry Kane sits on 10 World Cup goals, level with Gary Lineker as England's all-time WC top scorer. One more makes the record his alone.

The 2018 rematch: The only prior meeting was England 6–1 Panama in Russia — Kane hat-trick, Stones brace. No revenge on offer; Panama just want to land a blow.

Two Thomases: Tuchel vs Christiansen — a Champions League-stacked squad against a side built on MLS and Liga MX names plus 2018 veterans.

What To Watch For

England's creators vs Panama's low blockEliminated and proud, Panama will sit deep and pack the box — they conceded just one goal in each loss. The game is England's width and movement (Saka, Bellingham) trying to pick a lock. Watch for patience vs frustration.
Aníbal Godoy vs Jude BellinghamPanama's 36-year-old captain and record cap-holder screens the space Bellingham loves to attack. Veteran positioning against late runs between the lines — if Godoy plays.
Amir Murillo vs England's left sidePanama's most Europe-seasoned defender is also their best outlet going forward. Pinned back most of the night, his bursts in transition are one of the few avenues to threaten.
Díaz & Waterman vs England's high linePanama's only realistic route to a goal is pace on the counter. If England commit numbers chasing the win, this is where Panama can bite.
Panama dead-ball: Waterman is the aerial focal point; Bárcenas/Murillo on delivery. Worth noting — Panama's only-ever World Cup goal (2018) came from a set piece.
England dead-ball: A genuine weapon. Rice & Saka deliver; targets are Kane, Guéhi, the centre-backs and Dan Burn (one of the tournament's tallest) if he features. Kane takes penalties.
Set-piece tip: Against a packed defence, England's highest-percentage route is a set piece. Treat every England corner and wide free kick as a real chance.
