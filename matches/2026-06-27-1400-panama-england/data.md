## What To Watch For
- England's creators vs Panama's low block: Eliminated and proud, Panama will sit deep and pack the box — they conceded just one goal in each loss. The game is England's width and movement (Saka, Bellingham) trying to pick a lock. Patience vs frustration.
- Aníbal Godoy vs Jude Bellingham: Panama's 36-year-old captain and record cap-holder screens the space Bellingham loves to attack. Veteran positioning vs late runs between the lines — if Godoy plays.
- Amir Murillo vs England's left side: Panama's most Europe-seasoned defender is also their best outlet going forward. Pinned back most of the night, his bursts in transition are one of the few avenues to threaten.
- Díaz & Waterman vs England's high line: Panama's only realistic route to a goal is pace on the counter. If England commit numbers chasing the win, this is where Panama can bite.
- Panama dead-ball: Waterman is the aerial focal point; Bárcenas/Murillo on delivery. Panama's only-ever World Cup goal (2018) came from a set piece.
- England dead-ball: A genuine weapon. Rice & Saka deliver; targets are Kane, Guéhi, the centre-backs and Dan Burn (one of the tournament's tallest) if he features. Kane takes penalties.
- Set-piece tip: Against a packed defence, England's highest-percentage route is a set piece. Treat every England corner and wide free kick as a real chance.
