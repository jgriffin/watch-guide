## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **First-ever competitive meeting** between Colombia and Ghana — billed as the first official A-level match in their history; no real head-to-head to lean on.
- **The Queiroz reunion:** Ghana's coach **Carlos Queiroz managed Colombia (2019–21)** — he faces his old national team in a knockout and knows this Colombian core intimately. It's his **5th World Cup as a manager** (after Portugal, Iran), and he only took the Ghana job in **April 2026**.
- **2010 heartbreak:** Ghana came within a kick of the **first African World Cup semifinal** — Luis Suárez's deliberate handball on the line, then **Asamoah Gyan's last-minute penalty miss**, and Uruguay won the shootout. Ghana were the third African side to a WC quarterfinal (after Cameroon '90, Senegal '02).
- **Split-nation brothers:** **Iñaki Williams (Ghana)** and **Nico Williams (Spain)** — born in Bilbao to Ghanaian parents, the brothers play for **different national teams**; Iñaki even won a senior Spain cap before switching.
- **Ayew dynasty:** captain **Jordan Ayew** is the son of three-time African Footballer of the Year **Abedi Pele** and brother of André — a multi-generation Black Stars family.
- **In without their star:** Ghana reached the R32 **without Mohammed Kudus** (Tottenham), sidelined by injury — a knockout run built on depth, not their marquee name.
- **Colombia, group winners over Portugal:** topped Group K conceding **once in three games**, including their **first-ever 0-0 draw at a World Cup** (vs Ronaldo's Portugal) to clinch first.
- **Heavy favorites:** ~**98% of moneyline tickets** are on Colombia (−190); Ghana (+600) snuck through as a best-third-placed side — nothing to lose.
