## What To Watch For
- James between the lines vs Portugal's pivot: Colombia's attack runs through James in the pockets ahead of the back four. If João Neves & Vitinha snuff those half-spaces, Los Cafeteros go flat; if James gets time, the killer pass arrives.
- Ronaldo vs Davinson Sánchez & Lucumí: the duel that matters is cutting off the supply line from Bruno Fernandes. Deny the service and Ronaldo is starved; let one cross slip and he still punishes it.
- Luis Díaz vs João Cancelo: Díaz's directness and relentless pressing against Portugal's attack-minded full-back — a transition flashpoint both ways. Whoever wins this flank tilts the game.
- Nuno Mendes / Pedro Neto vs Daniel Muñoz: Portugal's dangerous left against Colombia's overlapping right-back. Lots of pace in a small space; likely source of the first big chance.
- Set-piece tip: If it's tight late, watch every Bruno Fernandes delivery into the box — Portugal carry more aerial threat and it's their cleanest route to a winner.
- Dead-ball (Colombia): James Rodríguez is the specialist — direct free kicks, corners, penalties; Jhon Arias on secondary delivery. Aerial targets Davinson Sánchez & Lucumí.
- Dead-ball (Portugal): Bruno Fernandes takes corners and free kicks (penalties Bruno/Ronaldo). Aerial mass through Ronaldo, Rúben Dias & Veiga — slight set-piece edge to Portugal.

- Ronaldo's last dance: 41 years old, a record 6th World Cup, captaining for the final time. The World Cup is the one major trophy he has never won. First man ever to score at six different World Cups; Portugal's outright all-time WC top scorer.
- James chasing Valderrama: 4 assists from Colombia's all-time assist record; a 10th World Cup cap would tie the Colombian record (Valderrama, Rincón).
- First-ever meeting: Colombia and Portugal have never played a senior international — no head-to-head to lean on.

- Rúben Neves wears No. 21 in tribute to Diogo Jota.
