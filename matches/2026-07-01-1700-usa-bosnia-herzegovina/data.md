## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **2013 friendly, Sarajevo: USA 4-3 Bosnia.** Jozy Altidore scored a second-half **hat-trick**, USA roaring back from 2-0 down — their first comeback win on European soil — to **snap Bosnia's 8-game unbeaten run** and stretch a then globe-leading 12-game US win streak. Džeko scored for Bosnia that day.
- **Bosnia have conceded in all 6 World Cup matches they have ever played** — and in 11 of their last 12 across all competitions. The clean sheet is mythical for them.
- **Bosnia eliminated 4× world champions Italy** in the UEFA playoff to reach 2026 — sending the Azzurri to a third straight World Cup absence.
- **Tim Weah's father George Weah** is the **only African player to win the Ballon d'Or** (1995) and later became **President of Liberia** — no other current WC player has a parent who led a nation.
- **Edin Džeko at 40** holds Bosnia's records for both goals (73) and caps (148) and became the **oldest goalscorer in 2. Bundesliga history** this season.
- **First competitive meeting ever:** all three prior USA–Bosnia games were friendlies (USA lead 2-0-1: 2013 W, 2018 0-0 D, 2021 W).
- **Bosnia & Herzegovina (~3.2M people)** would be one of the smallest nations in the last 16 if they advance — David vs the host Goliath (USA ~340M).
- **Pochettino's 9 changes** vs Türkiye were among the most a US side has ever made between two World Cup games — a deliberate knockout-prep rotation (rested Adams, Balogun, Richards, Robinson off yellows).
- **The golden generation's torch:** playmaker **Miralem Pjanić retired in Dec 2025**, leaving Džeko as the last man standing of the 2014 World Cup side.
