## What To Watch For
- **De Bruyne (BEL, free No.10) vs Senegal's midfield screen (I. Gueye / Camara / Diarra)** — the single most decisive duel. Belgium's whole attack runs through KDB; Senegal answer with a physical ball-winning midfield. Get a body on him and Belgium go blunt; give him a half-yard and Doku/Trossard run in behind.
- **Doku (BEL, winger) vs Senegal's right side (Diatta at RB)** — Doku is Belgium's most reliable spark, pure 1v1 acceleration; Diatta is a converted winger, not a natural full-back. If Senegal can't double him, this is Belgium's likeliest goal.
- **Mané + Ismaïla Sarr (SEN, pace in transition) vs Belgium's aging back line** — Belgium are slow at the back and have looked nervous all tournament. Senegal's plan is transition; Courtois behind them is the equalizer, but the moment Belgium over-commit it's where Senegal hurt them.
- **Belgium's quality/control vs Senegal's athleticism/chaos** — the macro-battle: a controlled Belgian grind, or an open broken-field track meet that suits Senegal.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball:** *Belgium edge on delivery* — **De Bruyne** takes pens, free kicks and corners (Lukaku backup pen), one of the world's best deliverers; with Mendy out, Senegal are a softer aerial proposition. *Senegal's best route is their own height* — CBs Niakhaté/Seck (and Koulibaly off the bench) are real corner targets. **Senegal penalties: Ismaïla Sarr, not Mané** (Mané stepped down after successive misses; Sarr ice-cold in finals since) — *re-confirm on the day.*
- **Tip:** In a tight tie watch every Belgian set-piece (De Bruyne's boot is their structural edge) and any Senegal attacking corner (their cheapest route past a slow back line and a backup keeper).

## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **First-ever World Cup meeting** — Belgium and Senegal have never met at a World Cup; any prior games were friendlies and sources disagree on the tally, so treat as a rare, essentially first competitive meeting. (*Flag: friendly count unreliable.*)
- **Two generations at the crossroads:** this is almost certainly the last World Cup for Belgium's core — De Bruyne (34), Lukaku (~33), Courtois (34) — the aging remnant of the side that took **third place at the 2018 World Cup**, the golden generation's best-ever finish. Garcia openly leaned on his "oldies" to escape the group.
- **Mané's farewell:** Senegal's greatest-ever player in his final World Cup, chasing the ghost of **Senegal 2002** — quarterfinalists (only the second African side to get that far, after Cameroon 1990), the run launched by a 1-0 opening upset of holders France.
- **A French coach vs a 2002 hero:** Rudi Garcia (French) faces Pape Thiaw, a member of that 2002 squad — he provided the assist for the golden goal that beat Sweden in the Round of 16.
- **Senegal's goalkeeper crisis:** first-choice **Édouard Mendy is out (knee)**, pushing Mory Diaw into a knockout against De Bruyne's delivery and Belgium's box threat — a genuine swing factor.
- **De Bruyne & Lukaku, club teammates again** — both moved to Napoli in 2025; Lukaku is Belgium's all-time leading scorer, De Bruyne his long-time chief supplier, and they line up together for country too.
- **The penalty baton passed:** Mané, a two-time successive spot-kick misser, ceded duties to **Ismaïla Sarr** — so a shootout would be decided by the *other* Sarr (Pape Matar is the midfielder).
- **Both captains are 34-year-old ex-Premier League/Serie A stars now in Saudi Arabia** — Mané (Al-Nassr) and Koulibaly (Al-Hilal).
- **Belgium came through the Egypt/Iran group** (Group G) — two cagey draws (1-1, 0-0) before a 5-1 explosion vs New Zealand — while Senegal took the hard road as a best-third-placed team out of a brutal Group I with France and Norway.
- **Raw, recent subplot:** several Senegal players (Mané, Pape Gueye, Mendy) featured in the chaotic **2025 AFCON final vs Morocco** (Jan 2026) — a Senegal 1-0 win on the night that CAF later overturned 3-0 to Morocco after a stoppage-time walk-off. *(Phrase carefully: 2021 AFCON title is clean; 2025 is disputed/stripped.)*
