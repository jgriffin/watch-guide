## What's At Stake & What's Next
- **Argentina:** Win = a place in the quarter-finals and the back-to-back dream stays alive — **no nation has retained the World Cup since Brazil in 1962.** Anything less than the final would be a disappointment for the champions.
- **Egypt:** Win = the greatest result in Egyptian football history — a **first-ever World Cup quarter-final.** They've already broken their knockout duck this tournament; every round now is new ground.
- **Next:** Winner → Quarter-final, **Sat Jul 11**, vs the winner of **Switzerland vs Colombia**.
- **Messi milestone:** at **39**, this is his **sixth and final World Cup**; he already holds the all-time records for **World Cup appearances (30)** and **World Cup goals (20)**. Likely his last World Cup knockout run — the story of the day.
- **Salah subplot:** Egypt's captain has been closing on **Hossam Hassan's all-time Egypt goal record (69)** — he was on **68** in the group stage, chasing the very man now sitting in the Egypt dugout as head coach.

## What To Watch For
- **Messi (ARG, 10) vs Egypt's low block** — Egypt will sit deep and try to crowd Messi with the Ashour/Fathy pivot dropping onto him and the centre-backs stepping. The whole game is Argentina patiently unpicking a packed box; the moment a gap opens for Messi to thread or curl one, it's danger. Egypt's plan lives or dies on their shape holding.
- **Salah (EGY, 10) vs Argentina's right side (Molina / Romero)** — Egypt's realistic route to a goal is Salah in transition. Argentina's full-backs push high; the instant Argentina lose the ball, Salah cutting in off the right onto his left foot is the counter Egypt want. Whether he's fully sharp is the sub-plot (managed a leg knock earlier in the tournament).
- **Midfield control: Enzo / Mac Allister / De Paul (ARG) vs Ashour / Fathy (EGY)** — Argentina will dominate the ball; the question is whether Egypt's double pivot can stay compact for 90+ minutes and spring the odd counter, or get overrun and pulled apart.
- **Lautaro & the strikers vs Egypt's centre-backs (Abdelmonem / Rabia)** — with Argentina controlling territory, Lautaro's movement and Álvarez off the bench test a back line that will spend long spells defending its own box.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball tip:** against a deep block, a dead ball may be Argentina's cleanest opening — **Romero, Otamendi and Lautaro** are aerial threats on **Messi / Mac Allister** delivery. At the other end, **Salah's right boot** on any Egyptian free kick is the one moment Egypt can manufacture from nothing. In a tight game, watch the corners.
