## What To Watch For
- **Messi (ARG, 10) vs Cape Verde's low block** — the whole match. Cape Verde will pack the middle and defend deep; Messi's job is to find the pockets between lines. Watch where he drifts — drop deep to face the ball, or hold high to attack the last gap.
- **Cape Verde's front three on the counter vs Argentina's high line** — the Blue Sharks' one real route. Mendes, Rodrigues and Benchimol springing in behind the moment Argentina over-commit, attacking the space Romero/Otamendi leave. If Cape Verde score, it likely looks like this.
- **Vozinha (CV, 1) vs the Argentine barrage** — the plan B that's really plan A. The 40-year-old keeper shut out Spain *and* Saudi Arabia in the group. Another inspired shift is Cape Verde's most realistic path to extra time.
- **Argentina's midfield (De Paul–Enzo–Mac Allister) vs Cape Verde's compact middle (Kevin Pina, Jamiro Monteiro, Laros Duarte)** — the control battle. Can a settled diaspora midfield stay disciplined and unbroken for 90 minutes against the world champions' engine room?
- **Set-piece / dead-ball:** Argentina hold a clear aerial edge (Otamendi, Romero, Lautaro) against a smaller Cape Verde — a likely route to break a stubborn block. Cape Verde's own dead balls (Rodrigues delivery) are a rare chance to trouble a tall back line. Penalties: Argentina lean Messi / Lautaro; **if it ever reaches a shootout, Emi Martínez is the trump card** (2022 shootout folk hero).
- **Tip:** If it's still 0-0 past the hour, watch for two things — Argentina turning to attacking set-pieces to crack the block, and any Cape Verde counter or wide free-kick, their cheapest way to steal a goal.

## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **Cape Verde are the smallest nation ever to reach a World Cup knockout round** — population ~525,000 (smaller than Wyoming). *(Not the smallest ever to play at a WC — Iceland 2018 (~340k) was smaller, but never advanced.)*
- **Cape Verde went unbeaten through the group** — the first newcomers to do so across three group games since **Senegal 2002**, and the first debutant nation to reach the knockouts since **Slovakia 2010**.
- They **advanced on three straight draws** — first nation to progress that way since **Chile 1998**.
- A **true diaspora side:** more Cape Verdeans live abroad than at home; the 26 are employed by ~26 clubs across ~14 nations, many born/raised in Portugal, the Netherlands and France. Of ~2,970 group minutes, only ~256 came from players at top-100 clubs — the underdog's underdog.
- The archipelago of 10 islands won **independence from Portugal in 1975** and only joined FIFA in **1986** — to a debut-tournament knockout berth.
- **Argentina chase back-to-back titles** — no nation has defended the World Cup since **Brazil in 1962**.
- **Messi's last dance:** 19 career WC goals (all-time leader) and the **first player ever to score in 7 consecutive World Cup matches**. Every minute could be his last on the stage.
- Argentina **won Group J with a perfect record** (three wins, no goals conceded across the first two games); Cape Verde **conceded only twice** in the group while holding Spain scoreless.
