## What To Watch For
- **Erling Haaland vs Brazil's centre-backs (Marquinhos & Gabriel Magalhães).** The defining duel. Norway's entire plan is to get Haaland isolated and into the box on minimal service — 5 goals already this tournament. Brazil's high defensive line is exactly the space he punishes on the counter.
- **Vinícius Jr vs Norway's right side.** With Neymar sidelined, Vini is unambiguously Brazil's talisman (4 group goals). Norway's right-back has to defend him 1v1 without leaving the space Haaland needs on the break — the game's central tension.
- **Ødegaard vs Brazil's midfield.** Norway's creative hub must find the lanes to spring Haaland and Sørloth; with **Paquetá ruled out (hamstring)**, both engine rooms are reshuffled — whoever controls the middle third decides whether this is open or a low-block grind.
- **Set-piece box duels.** Norway are one of the tallest sides in the tournament — **Haaland (1.94m) and Sørloth (1.94m)** with Ødegaard delivering. Their clearest route to goal against a more technical opponent (echoes of Rekdal's 1998 winner, won from a set-piece scramble).
- **Set-piece / dead-ball tip:** Treat every Norway corner and wide free kick as a genuine chance — height + Ødegaard's delivery is how the underdog steals one. Brazil's threat is the opposite: open-play transition and 1v1 quality, not dead balls. If it's tight past the hour, Norway's set-pieces and a Brazil counter are the two likeliest ways a goal arrives. ## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **Brazil have NEVER beaten Norway** — winless in all four senior meetings (2 draws, 2 Norway wins). The hoodoo headline.
- **First Brazil–Norway World Cup meeting since the 1998 shock** — 28 years on, and the first competitive rematch of the group-stage upset that beat the reigning champions.
- **Norway's first World Cup since 1998** — they missed **seven straight** tournaments (2002 through 2022), a 28-year absence; this whole generation had never played a WC.
- **Norway's R32 win was their first-ever World Cup knockout WIN.** They reached the Round of 16 in 1938 and 1998 but lost both (to Italy each time). A quarterfinal would be a national first.
- **Haaland's World Cup debut tournament** — the world's most feared striker is playing his first-ever World Cup at 25, and already has **5 goals**, including Norway's decisive 86th-minute R32 winner.
- **Brazil's title drought:** 5-time champions but **no World Cup since 2002** — their longest-ever wait — chasing a record-extending 6th star under first foreign coach Ancelotti.
- **MetLife hosts the 2026 Final (Jul 19)** — this Round of 16 is a dress-rehearsal on the tournament's showpiece stage; the winner is two wins from returning here.
- **Changing of the guard:** with **Neymar** sidelined by a calf injury for most of the tournament, **Vinícius Jr has inherited the No.1 role** for the Seleção.
- **Ødegaard–Haaland connection:** the captain has assisted Haaland more than any other teammate across Haaland's Norway career.
