What It Meant & What's Next

Argentina: the win booked a place in the final vs Spain and kept alive the bid to retain the World Cup — no nation has done it since Brazil in 1962. Messi's sixth and final World Cup runs on to the last game.

England: a fourth semi-final near-miss of the modern era — into the third-place playoff vs France, still chasing a first men's trophy since 1966.

Messi milestone: at 39, this was his sixth and final World Cup; already all-time record-holder for WC appearances (30) and goals (20) — and now one more final.

Kane subplot: England's all-time top scorer, and now all-time WC top scorer past Lineker this tournament, came up one round short of the final.

The final on the horizon: Argentina vs Spain — reigning World Cup holders against the reigning European champions.

The Duels That Shaped It

Bellingham vs Argentina's midfield threeThe game inside the game. England's engine trying to break the champions' rhythm and feed Kane, against Enzo–Mac Allister–De Paul controlling tempo. Whoever owned the middle owned the tie.
Kane vs Romero & Lisandro MartínezKane dropping to link then attacking the box, against Argentina's aggressive, front-foot centre-backs. His duel with Cristian Romero was the physical heart of the match.
Saka vs Tagliafico & Argentina's leftEngland's likeliest spark, cutting in off the right onto his left foot. Whether Argentina's left side could handle him one-on-one was England's clearest route to goal.
Messi vs England's double pivotMessi roaming free off the front line; Rice's job to screen the space he drifts into. One moment of Messi magic is always the swing factor in a tight knockout — and it swung Argentina's way.
England dead-ball: a genuine set-piece side — Rice deliveries onto Kane, Guéhi, Konsa, Stones; Kane on penalties.
Argentina dead-ball: Messi / Mac Allister delivery to aerial threats Romero, Otamendi and Lautaro.
The margin: two extra-time survivors in the quarters met fresh off nervy wins — and in a 2-1 semi-final, the fine margins fell to the reigning champions.
