## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **The Azteca is the first stadium ever to host matches at three different World Cups** — 1970, 1986 and 2026. It staged Brazil's 1970 final win and Maradona's 1986.
- **Same ground as the "Hand of God" and "Goal of the Century."** On 22 June 1986, in the Azteca quarter-final, Maradona knocked England out with those two goals four minutes apart. England return to the exact venue 40 years later — different opponent, same ghosts. (England players have said they're "not seeking revenge.")
- **Mexico won all three group games for the first time in their World Cup history — and conceded zero group-stage goals.**
- **The 40-year curse just broke.** Mexico had gone out in the Round of 16 at **seven straight** World Cups (1994–2018), the nation's "quinto partido" obsession (never reaching a fifth game since 1986). The R32 win over Ecuador was their first knockout win in 40 years — beat England and it's a first quarter-final since 1986, on home soil.
- **Altitude ~7,200 ft** — the highest venue at any World Cup since 1986; a real physiological test for a sea-level England side (altitude prep has been a genuine talking point in the English press).
- **Kane's tournament:** passed Lineker to become England's all-time World Cup top scorer, and rescued the DR Congo tie with a late brace after England trailed from the 7th minute.
- **England have not won a World Cup since 1966** — and their only previous World Cup meeting with Mexico was a 2-0 group-stage win at Wembley that same year (1966).
- **Head-to-head, England lead 6-2-1** in nine meetings — but **Mexico won the last time the sides met in Mexico City (1-0, 1985)** and England have never beaten Mexico at the Azteca.
- **A 23-year age gap in one squad:** Ochoa (40, record 6th World Cup) and Mora (17, youngest Mexican ever at a World Cup) are both in Aguirre's group.
