## What To Watch For
- **Rafael Leão / Francisco Conceição vs a patched-up Croatia back line** — Portugal's pace on the flanks against defenders managing minutes (Gvardiol back from a broken leg; Perišić, 37, filling in at left-back). If Croatia sit in, the Portuguese wingers get isolation 1v1s — their cleanest route to a goal.
- **Vitinha & João Neves vs Modrić–Kovačić** — the midfield chess match, and the whole tie. Portugal's young PSG pivot press and carry; Croatia's two old masters slow the game to a walk and pick the killer pass. Whoever controls tempo controls the night.
- **Bruno Fernandes vs Croatia's deep block** — with Croatia likely happy to absorb, Bruno's set-piece delivery and through-balls are Portugal's pick-lock. Snuff his service and Ronaldo starves.
- **Ronaldo (41) vs Modrić (40)** — not a direct duel, but the headline: two all-time greats, both ~40, almost certainly at their final World Cup, in what's plausibly their last-ever meeting on a pitch.
- **Set-piece tip:** Croatia's most realistic path to a goal is a dead ball — **Modrić's delivery set up the Ghana winner**, and they carry aerial mass (Budimir, Gvardiol, Pongračić, Perišić). In a tight tie, treat every Croatian corner or wide free-kick as a genuine chance.
- **Dead-ball (Portugal):** Bruno Fernandes on corners & free-kicks; penalties Bruno / Ronaldo. Aerial threat through Ronaldo, Rúben Dias.
- **Dead-ball (Croatia):** Modrić is the primary deliverer (FKs, corners); Perišić the left-foot alternate. Penalties shared historically among Modrić / Kramarić / Perišić — *not firmly confirmed; flag.* Aerial targets: Budimir, Gvardiol, Pongračić, Perišić.

## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **First-ever World Cup meeting** between these two — despite ~9 prior friendlies / Nations League games. Portugal dominate the all-time H2H **5W–3D–1L**; Croatia's only win was **2-1 in Lisbon (June 2024 friendly)**.
- **Ronaldo (41) vs Modrić (40):** two of the defining players of their generation, both ~40, almost certainly at their final World Cup — plausibly their last-ever meeting on a pitch. Every preview runs the "old masters' farewell" billing.
- **Ronaldo is the first player ever to score at six different World Cups** (1934–2026 span of the tournament aside, his own span 2006→2026), and at 41 the oldest outfield protagonist of the tournament.
- **Modrić, 40,** is among the oldest outfielders ever at a World Cup — the spine of a team built entirely around his longevity.
- **Croatia are serial overachievers** for a nation of ~3.9 million: **2018 World Cup runners-up** (lost the final to France) and **2022 third place** (beat Morocco in the third-place game). Back-to-back deep runs.
- **Portugal arrive as group runners-up** after a flat 0-0 with Colombia — the −130 favorites tag sits awkwardly next to questions about their attacking sharpness, exactly the gap Croatia's experience is built to exploit.
- **Croatia recovered from a 4-2 opening-day battering by England** to win their next two — a side that grows into tournaments.
