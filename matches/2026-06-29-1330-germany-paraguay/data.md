## What To Watch For
- **Wirtz & Musiala (GER) vs Cubas + the Paraguay block.** The whole game: can Germany's two No.10s unpick a packed defense, or get frustrated into it (as vs Ecuador)? Cubas is Paraguay's screen — step on them before they turn.

- **Kimmich (GER, capt, RB/mid) vs Paraguay's counters.** Germany's tempo-setter and right-side supply; Paraguay target the space he vacates pushing up.
- **Gustavo Gómez (PAR capt, CB) vs Havertz in both boxes.** Two-way: Gómez must win the physical fight vs Havertz, and is Paraguay's biggest set-piece header threat at the other end.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball tip:** Germany are tall and dangerous from delivery — Kimmich in-swingers onto Rüdiger/Tah/Havertz (Havertz the likely penalty taker). Paraguay are more dead-ball-*reliant* (it's how a low-scoring side manufactures goals) — Gómez the aerial weapon, Almirón/Enciso deliver. If it's tight late, treat every Gómez set-piece as a real chance. (Alderete, another aerial target, is a knee doubt.)

## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **First-ever World Cup Round of 32** — this round didn't exist before the 48-team 2026 expansion; neither nation has played a match at this stage.
- Only previous WC meeting: **Germany 1-0 Paraguay, 2002 R16** (Oliver Neuville, 88') — Germany rode it to the final. They have never lost to Paraguay at a World Cup.
- Germany are the **only four-time world champions ever to exit in the group stage in consecutive tournaments** (2018, 2022) — a ghost they just exorcised by topping Group E.
- Germany opened by **putting seven past debutants Curaçao (7-1)**, then lost a dead rubber to Ecuador with the group already won.
- Paraguay advanced on just **4 points and −2 GD** as a best third-placed team — conceded four to the USA, then kept clean sheets vs Türkiye and Australia.
- **Paraguay's first World Cup since 2010** — a 16-year gap; 2010 (quarterfinals, lost 1-0 to eventual champs Spain) is still their best-ever run.
- **Alfaro's revival:** undefeated in 11 of their last 12 qualifiers, beating both **Brazil (1-0) and Argentina (2-1)** to grab the last automatic CONMEBOL spot.
- **Manuel Neuer is the last active member of Germany's 2014 World Cup-winning squad**, having reversed his international retirement.
- **Leroy Sané** now plays club football in Turkey for **Galatasaray** (left Bayern on a free in 2025).
- **Jamal Musiala** is playing roughly a year on from a fractured fibula and dislocated ankle at the 2025 Club World Cup.
