What's At Stake & What's Next

Germany: Win and the redemption arc continues — they're the only four-time champions ever to suffer back-to-back group exits (2018, 2022). Lose and it's a third straight tournament disaster under a young, already-extended Nagelsmann.

Paraguay: Their first World Cup since 2010 — and a chance to reach the knockout business end for the first time since that quarterfinal run. House money for Alfaro's overachievers; everything from here is a bonus.

Winner advances to the Round of 16 — likely vs the winner of France vs Sweden (to be confirmed), at Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia.

The 2002 rematch: Their only previous World Cup meeting was the 2002 Round of 16 — Germany 1-0 on an 88th-minute Oliver Neuville goal, on the way to the final. Germany have never lost to Paraguay at a World Cup.

Style clash: Germany's possession and two No.10s (Wirtz, Musiala) vs a Paraguay side built on grit — two group clean sheets, beat both Brazil and Argentina in qualifying.

What To Watch For

Wirtz & Musiala vs Cubas + the blockThe whole game: can Germany's two No.10s unpick a packed Paraguay defense, or get frustrated into it (as they were vs Ecuador)? Andrés Cubas is the screen who steps on them before they turn.
Rüdiger / Tah vs Sanabria + EncisoParaguay's goals come on the break — Sanabria holds it up, Enciso bursts in behind. Rüdiger's recovery pace against Enciso is the pivotal transition duel. (Schlotterbeck is out injured, so it's Tah–Rüdiger.)
Kimmich vs Paraguay's countersGermany's captain and tempo-setter supplies the right side; Paraguay will target the space he vacates when he pushes up from full-back.
Gustavo Gómez vs Havertz, both boxesTwo-way duel: Paraguay's captain must win the physical fight against Havertz at one end — and is their biggest set-piece header threat at the other.
Set-piece tip: Germany are tall and dangerous from delivery — watch Kimmich in-swingers onto Rüdiger, Tah and Havertz (the likely penalty taker). But dead balls are also Paraguay's best route: if it's tight late, treat every Gustavo Gómez set-piece as a genuine chance.
