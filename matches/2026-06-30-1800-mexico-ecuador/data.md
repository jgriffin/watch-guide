## What To Watch For

- **Mexico's centre-forward (Jiménez) vs Pacho + Hincapié** — strength vs the wall. Ecuador's young CB pairing — **Willian Pacho (PSG)**, a qualifying blocks leader, and **Piero Hincapié (Arsenal)** — anchors a defense that conceded just **5 goals in 18 qualifiers**. ## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **The Azteca is the only stadium to host two World Cup finals** — 1970 (Brazil 4-1 Italy) and 1986 (Argentina 3-2 West Germany). With 2026 it becomes the **first venue to feature in three different World Cups.**
- It's the stage of **two of football's most famous moments**, both in the 1986 Argentina–England quarterfinal here: Maradona's **"Hand of God"** and his **"Goal of the Century."**
- **Altitude ~2,240 m (≈7,200 ft):** the thin air saps visitors' late-game legs and speeds the ball — a real home-field weapon against sea-level sides.
- **Mexico's "El Quinto Partido" curse:** seven straight Round-of-16 exits (1994–2018) and out in the group in 2022. Their only two quarterfinals ever came as hosts — **1970 and 1986, both at this stadium.** Tonight is the quest to finally reach the elusive "fifth game."
- **Mexico's last World Cup knockout win was in 1986** — beating Bulgaria at this very ground. A 40-year wait to win another on the same turf.
- **Mexico's group was watertight:** three wins, **zero goals conceded** — one of very few hosts to manage a perfect, clean-sheet group.
- **Ecuador conceded just 5 goals in 18 qualifiers** (about half the next-best, Argentina) and kept **13 clean sheets** — the meanest defense in South America; in their last 11 qualifiers they conceded one goal.
- **Ecuador spent only ~97 minutes losing across the entire 18-game qualifying campaign.**
- **First World Cup meeting since 2002** and only the second ever — Mexico won the first **2-1 (2002 group stage)**; Ecuador have never beaten Mexico at a World Cup. All-time the series is lopsided: ~25 meetings, **Mexico ~14 W, Ecuador 4, 7 D**; last meeting a 1-1 friendly in Oct 2025.
- **Enner Valencia** is Ecuador's all-time top scorer and has scored at multiple World Cups — a milestone-watch every time he plays.
