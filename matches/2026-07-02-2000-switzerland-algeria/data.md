## What To Watch For
- **Dan Ndoye (SUI, RW) vs Rayan Aït-Nouri (ALG, LB)** — Switzerland's most direct dribbler at the side Algeria must protect. If Ndoye isolates his man 1v1 he's a chance-creation engine; Aït-Nouri (Man City) also wants to push forward, so the space behind him is the game.
- **Mohamed Amoura's pace vs the Swiss back line / Akanji** — Algeria's whole plan is springing Amoura in behind a Swiss high-ish line; Switzerland's compact block exists to deny exactly that. Akanji's recovery pace is the key matchup. *Hinges on Amoura's fitness (thigh) — see below.*
- **Granit Xhaka vs Riyad Mahrez** — the two captains and creative hubs. Whoever owns the central tempo owns the game: Xhaka dictating from deep vs Mahrez finding pockets between the lines.
- **Midfield control: Xhaka–Freuler–Manzambi vs Algeria's double pivot (Zerrouki + Boudaoui) & Bentaleb** — Switzerland want possession and to starve Algeria; Algeria want turnovers and transition. The middle third decides whether this is a Swiss grind or an open counter-game.
- **Set-piece / dead-ball:** *Switzerland* — pens **Xhaka** (then Embolo, Amdouni); free kicks Vargas/Xhaka/Rieder; corners Vargas/Rieder/Rodríguez (*Vargas delivery primary but fitness-questionable — flag*). *Algeria* — pens & free kicks **Mahrez** (and Chaïbi); corners Chaïbi/Mahrez/Aouar. Mahrez's delivery is Algeria's most reliable dead-ball weapon.
- **Tip:** In a likely Swiss-controlled grind, treat any Algeria set piece or quick transition as their cheapest route to a goal — and watch the first ball over the top to Amoura/Gouiri.

## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **The man in Algeria's dugout built this Swiss team.** Vladimir Petković coached **Switzerland 2014–2021** — 2018 World Cup R16, and **beating France 5-4 on penalties at Euro 2020** for their first-ever Euro QF — before taking the Algeria job in Feb 2024. He faces his old nation in the knockouts.
- **72 years and counting:** a win sends Switzerland to the quarter-finals for the **first time since 1954** — when, as hosts, their run ended in a **7-5 loss to Austria**, still the highest-scoring match in World Cup history.
- **Algeria's 12-year exile is over** — their first World Cup since **2014** (missed 2018 and 2022); their 5th appearance ever (1982, 1986, 2010, 2014).
- **Xhaka is now Switzerland's joint-record World Cup appearance holder** (13 caps, level with teammate Ricardo Rodríguez), reached vs Qatar.
- **Amoura led all of Africa in qualifying goals** — 10 in the campaign, including the penalties that clinched qualification.
- **Mahrez almost single-handedly delivered the knockouts** — his brace in the 3-3 vs Austria pushed Algeria through as a best-third team. Likely his last World Cup at 35.
- **Switzerland is a knockout specialist:** beat France (Euro 2020) and reigning champions Italy (Euro 2024) in recent tournament knockouts — but have **exited in the R16 in four of the last five World Cups.**
- **Algeria has never beaten Switzerland** (0 wins in 2 meetings, both 1980s friendlies); this is their first-ever competitive clash.
- **Contrasting routes in:** Switzerland won Group B clean (incl. a 4-1 of Bosnia); Algeria scraped through Group J as a best third behind Argentina and Austria, conceding 3 in their last game.
- **Watch the kid:** Switzerland's **Johan Manzambi** has caught fire (multiple goal involvements late in the group stage) and is in Young Player Award talk — the in-form spark of an otherwise veteran spine.
