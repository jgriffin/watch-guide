## Fun Facts / Quirks
- **They've met at a World Cup before — on this exact date.** Netherlands beat Morocco **2-1 at the 1994 World Cup group stage**, also played **June 29** (Bergkamp & Bryan Roy scored). This tie is June 29, 2026 — same calendar date, 32 years later.
- **All three senior meetings ever were one-goal games:** 1994 WC NED 2-1 · 1999 friendly MAR 2-1 · 2017 friendly NED 2-1. Netherlands lead the H2H **2-1**.
- First **competitive** meeting since 1994; only their fourth meeting all-time; last meeting was a May 2017 friendly.
- **Morocco are the defending semifinalists** — in 2022 the first African and first Arab nation ever to reach a World Cup semifinal (beat Belgium, Spain on pens, Portugal 1-0; lost to France).
- **New regime:** 2022 architect Walid Regragui left in March 2026; **Mohamed Ouahbi** — who won the **2025 U-20 World Cup** (beat Argentina 2-0 in the final) — now leads a strikingly young side (teen/early-20s Bouaddi, El Khannouss).
- **Hakimi's 2022 signature** was a panenka penalty straight down the middle to eliminate Spain in the shootout.
- **Hakimi's 2025-26 was historic** — PSG Champions League (scored in the 5-0 final win over old club Inter), Ligue 1, Super Cup; ~3 goals, 16 assists on the season.
- **Memphis Depay**, the Netherlands' record goalscorer, now plays in Brazil for **Corinthians** — a quirk in a squad otherwise full of top-five-league names.
- Netherlands won Group F with 10 goals; Morocco finished group play **unbeaten**, 2nd only behind Brazil on goal difference.
