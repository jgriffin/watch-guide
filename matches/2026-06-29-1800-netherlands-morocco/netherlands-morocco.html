What's At Stake & What's Next

Win = Round of 16 in Houston on Sat Jul 4, most likely vs the South Africa / Canada winner (to be confirmed); a projected quarter-final path toward France or Germany.

Lose = elimination. A bracket quirk pitted two strong sides together this early — one of them takes a shock exit in the round of 32.

Morocco's new era: 2022 semi-final architect Walid Regragui left in March; Mohamed Ouahbi — fresh off winning the 2025 U-20 World Cup — now leads a strikingly young side (En-Nesyri and Ziyech left out).

The heritage subplot: many of Morocco's stars were born and raised in the Netherlands and chose the Atlas Lions — Dutch-born Noussair Mazraoui lines up against the country he could have represented.

Hakimi's moment: fresh off a Champions League title and Ballon d'Or buzz, he captains Morocco in a knockout on North American soil — the form star of the tie on either side.

32 years to the day: these teams' only World Cup meeting was a 2-1 Dutch win on June 29, 1994 — replayed on the same calendar date tonight.

What To Watch For

Hakimi vs GakpoThe marquee flank duel — both want the same touchline. Hakimi bombs forward like a winger; Gakpo is the Dutch left-side danger. Push high, Achraf, and Gakpo gets the space in behind you.
Frenkie de Jong vs Morocco's pressThe control battle. Morocco's young double pivot (Bouaddi & El Aynaoui) swarms the buildup; De Jong's carry under pressure is the Dutch escape valve. Smother him and the Netherlands stall.
Mazraoui vs Dumfries & MalenThe heritage flank: Dutch-born Mazraoui defending the right against the country he could have played for, with Dumfries the Dutch attacking outlet behind Malen.
Van Dijk vs a strikerless MoroccoA movement battle, not an aerial one. With no classic No.9, Morocco run Saibari as a false-9 and Brahim Díaz between the lines — Van Dijk must track runners, not win headers.
Netherlands dead-ball: the clear aerial power — Van Dijk, Van de Ven, Van Hecke, Brobbey & Dumfries make a tall, dangerous box. Penalties likely Depay (if on) or Gakpo/Reijnders.
Morocco dead-ball: Hakimi is the iconic spot-kick man (panenka that knocked out Spain in 2022) and a delivery threat from the right.
Set-piece tip: In a tight, low-scoring tie, Dutch attacking corners and free kicks are their biggest structural edge — and Hakimi's deliveries are Morocco's cheapest route back into it. Watch every dead ball near the box.
