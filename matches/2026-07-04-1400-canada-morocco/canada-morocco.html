What It Meant & What's Next

Morocco: Through to the Quarter-final — back in the last eight, three years after becoming the first African/Arab nation to reach a World Cup semi-final. Next: France, Thu Jul 9, a straight rematch of that 2022 semi (France won 2–0). Revenge on offer.

Canada: Out — but this was their best-ever World Cup. They'd already made history in the R32, winning the first World Cup knockout match in Canadian men's history (1–0 vs South Africa). The Round of 16 is uncharted, celebrated territory for Les Rouges.

The result matched the paper: Morocco (FIFA ~#7, 2022 semifinalists) were heavy favorites over Canada (~#32). A comfortable 3–0 was about what the gap suggested.

Goal detail: the 3–0 scoreline is confirmed; individual scorers/minutes aren't recorded in the available data, so this sheet doesn't attribute the goals. Morocco's quality simply told across 90 minutes.

What Decided It

The quality gap, plainlyA Champions-League-winning captain (Hakimi) and a Real Madrid playmaker (Brahim Díaz) against plucky first-time knockout winners. Over 90 minutes the class told and Morocco controlled the game.
Morocco's press & midfield ownershipMorocco's young, high-energy engine room — Bouaddi, El Aynaoui, El Khannouss — swarmed Canada's first pass and denied Marsch's side the platform they'd used to beat South Africa. Canada rarely settled on the ball.
Hakimi's flank vs Canada's leftCanada's biggest pre-match hope was pace down the left — but with Alphonso Davies still a fitness spectator, that threat never came, and Hakimi was free to attack like an auxiliary winger.
No Davies spark, David starvedThe one Canadian who could have turned it into a shoot-out spent the whole tournament nursing his post-ACL recovery. Without him, Jonathan David got no service and Canada lacked a game-breaker to trouble Morocco's back line.
Dead-ball footnote: Morocco carried the set-piece edge too — Hakimi the penalty king (2022 panenka to knock out Spain; scored the R32 shootout winner vs the Netherlands). Canada's aerial route through Larin never got going against a settled Moroccan defence.
